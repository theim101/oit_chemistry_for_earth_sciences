## Introduction

This week will hlearn how to use the principles of redox chemistry to draw Pourbaix diagrams, also known as Eh-pH or pE-pH diagrams. These are very useful to graphically represent the stable phases (in the liquid or solid) at a certain combination of redox potential and pH. These types of diagrams, also referred to as stability diagrams, are commonly used in geochemistry.
