This example ORCHESTRA system demonstrates how to implement the PHREEQC system explained by Tony Appelo on his site:

https://hydrochemistry.eu/exmpls/ca_f.html

The simulation starts with calculating soil water (SOLUTION 1), with a CO2 pressure of 0.1 atm, 
and Ca and F concentrations in equilibrium with calcite and fluorite, respectively. 

This results in a solution containing 155 mg Ca/L and 2.17 mg F/L. (with a recent version of PHREEQC)
Next, 7.5 mmol Na-feldspar dissolve in 15 steps (keyword REACTION), 
while calcite, fluorite, kaolinite and quartz react to equilibrium (keyword EQUILIBRIUM_PHASES).

The PHREEQC definition looks as follows:

SOLUTION 1
 pH 7 charge
 C 1 CO2(g) -1
 Ca 1 Calcite
 F 1 Fluorite

REACTION
 NaAlSi3O8 1
 7.5e-3 in 15
 
EQUILIBRIUM_PHASES 1
 Fluorite
 Calcite
 Quartz 0 0
 Kaolinite 0 0
